Salary by Age

Salary growth is rarely linear, and age-based benchmarks help explain why. At earlier stages, income ranges are tighter and movement between percentiles can happen quickly. By the mid-30s and 40s, the spread between the median and top quartile becomes much wider. These pages let you compare your current salary with typical earnings for the same age band, then jump directly into percentile views for practical context. Use these age pages to identify whether your pay is near the median, ahead of peers, or lagging, and to set the next realistic target salary range for your career stage.
