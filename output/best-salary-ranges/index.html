Best Salary Ranges

There is no one universal “best” salary, but some ranges tend to offer a stronger balance between purchasing power and percentile rank for a wide set of age groups. This guide helps you compare major salary bands and quickly branch into the exact percentile pages for your age and state. If your compensation is below your target band, use these pages to estimate the next step that meaningfully improves your rank. If your salary is already in a higher band, the same pages help you track progress against top-quartile and top-decile thresholds rather than guessing from national averages alone. Start with your closest bracket, then check nearby ranges to understand how much additional income is typically needed to move up in percentile terms for your stage of career.
